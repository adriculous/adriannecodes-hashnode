Meant to be used as a blog covering data analytics and web design, at the end of the day, this Hashnode version ended up being abandoned, collecting dust, and somehow, I even forgot that my domain, `adrianne.codes` became a redirected domain to this blog.

For the past two year, **Adrianne Codes** has lived happily on Hashnode — my cozy corner of the internet where I shared coding notes, web dev experiments, and random bursts of pastel tech energy. 🩵

But as my projects (and curiosity) keep growing, it’s time for Adrianne Codes to revert back— and evolve— into something *bigger* — a full web development portfolio and tutorial site that reflects both sides of me: the developer 👩🏻‍💻 and the designer 🌸.

---

## ⚙️ Why the Change?

Maybe I’m still unfamiliar with all of Hashnode’s features and capabilities, but somehow, the thought of having little to no control on my blogging workflow steered me away from continuously blogging on Hashnode. I don’t even remember if Hashnode even had a mobile app, so that I can just write my drafts and publish them without relying on my laptop.

I wanted a space that feels:

* **More flexible** — where I can organize tutorials, projects, and case studies without being tied to a single blog format.
    
* **More personal** — my own domain, my own banners, my own design language.
    
* **More connected** — a home that links to my AI, data, and creative sites (like my new portfolio portal, \[Portal by Adrianne\](https://adrianne.io) and its subsites).
    

Hashnode has been amazing, but I’m craving that *independent portfolio energy*. ⚖️

---

## 🧱 The New Framework

I’ll be rebuilding [**adrianne.codes**](http://adrianne.codes) as a stand-alone website using:

* 🩵 **Notion** — for easy editing and content management (no need to push from laptop!)
    
* 💎 [**Super.so**](http://Super.so) — to publish it beautifully, with custom CSS, banners, and my own domain
    
* 🧰 **GitHub (**`adriannecodes-hashnode`) — to keep automatic backups of all my Hashnode posts
    

So yes — Adrianne Codes will soon be **a stand-alone dev portfolio + tutorial hub**, powered by Notion’s simplicity and [Super.so](http://Super.so)’s clean design. ✨

---

## 🎨 What to Expect

You’ll still find:

* Blog-style dev diaries (because I’ll never stop writing 🐰)
    
* Step-by-step tutorials for web tools, frameworks, and ethical coding practices
    
* Showcases of my projects — from static site builds to AI-assisted design experiments
    
* A bit of personality — bunnies, sparkles, and pastel blue still included 💖
    

---

## 💭 Closing Thoughts

This isn’t a goodbye to Hashnode — it’s more like a graduation.  
Hashnode helped me *start*. But now, **Adrianne Codes** is ready to *stand on its own two feet (and paws)*. 🐾✨ … again.

If you’re planning your own dev site or thinking of going indie with Notion or [Super.so](http://Super.so), stay tuned — I’ll be documenting the whole journey here and on the new site!

As for this Hashnode version? It won’t be called *Adrianne Codes* anymore. It will probably just a general Hashnode blog using my Hashnode URL, and it will be consisting of entries about anything about coding, design, AI, and so on.

Here’s to our new journeys!
